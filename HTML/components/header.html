Classes

Class / attributePurpose
.site-headerWrapper. Holds the optional top bar and the navbar.
.is-transparent / .is-darkVariants for use over a hero image or on a dark bar.
data-stickyKeeps the header at the top. site.js adds .is-scrolled for the shadow.
.site-topbarSlim strip for phone, email and social links.
.site-logo + .site-logo-markText logo. Swap for your own SVG or image.
.site-menuDesktop menu. Dropdowns use Bootstrap's dropdown JS.
.site-actionsButtons on the right.
