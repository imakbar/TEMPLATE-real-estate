Alert and toast

Messages for success, errors and tips, inline or as a temporary notification.

Examples

Toasts

Saved to favoritesFind it later in your wishlist.
Could not sendPlease try again in a moment.

Add data-toast="#id" to a button. Toasts appear in the bottom corner and close after a few seconds.

Classes

ClassPurpose
.alert .alert-success -danger -info -warningInline messages. Add role="alert".
.toast + .toast-success / -danger / -warningShort notification in a .toast-container.
data-toastOpens a toast from a button (site.js).
