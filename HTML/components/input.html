Classes

ClassPurpose
.form-controlText input. Sizes .form-control-sm (36) and .form-control-lg (52), default 44 px.
.input-iconWrapper that puts an icon inside an input. Add .icon-end to the icon to place it on the right.
.input-group + .input-group-textPrefix or suffix text such as a currency or unit.
.is-invalid / .is-validValidation states. Pair with .invalid-feedback or .valid-feedback.
.form-label / .form-textLabel above the field and help text below it.

Always connect a <label> to its field with for and id, and link help or error text with aria-describedby. Without a visible label, use aria-label.
