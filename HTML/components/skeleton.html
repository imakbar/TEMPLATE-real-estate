.skeletonShimmering placeholder base.

.skeleton-title, .skeleton-circle, .skeleton-image, .skeleton-buttonHeading, avatar, 4:3 image and button shapes.

Mark the loading region with aria-busy="true" and the placeholders with aria-hidden="true". The shimmer stops for users who prefer reduced motion.
