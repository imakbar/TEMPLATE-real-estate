First-time buyer checklist: ten things to check before you make an offer

  • Sara Ahmed
  • Mar 12, 2026
  • 6 min read

Buying your first home is exciting and a little scary. Use this list to slow down, look carefully and make an offer you feel good about.

1. Know your real budget

Add the deposit, taxes, legal fees and moving costs to the price. Then keep a buffer for repairs in the first year.

2. Check the structure

Look at the roof, walls and foundations. Damp patches, cracks and new paint in odd places are worth asking about.

A home that feels right on paper should also feel right at 8 pm on a Tuesday.

3. Visit at different times

Go once in the morning and once in the evening. Notice noise, parking and how much light the rooms get.

Before you offer

  • Ask how long it has been on the market.
  • Ask what is included in the sale.
  • Check the neighbourhood plans.

Take your time. A good agent will never rush you.
